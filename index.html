1. Summary

Afterlife ("the App") records, transcribes, and helps you study sermons. It is built so that the things you create — recordings, transcripts, notes, highlights, bookmarks, saved moments, search queries, and the scripture you read — stay on your device.

We do not operate an account system. We never ask you to sign in, and we do not know your identity.

The App does use the network for four narrowly defined purposes, described in full in Section 4: downloading the on-device speech model, Apple's own speech assets, processing your App Store purchase, and usage statistics and crash reporting that help us understand and improve the App.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the App contains no advertising identifier and no advertising SDK.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses the App on iOS or iPadOS. It does not cover the App Store itself, which is operated by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

3. What stays on your device

The following is created by you and is stored only in the App's local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us, and we have no ability to access, read, or recover it:

  • Sermon audio recordings and imported audio files
  • Transcripts, including live and corrected transcripts
  • Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, and saved moments
  • Playlists, memory cards, and review history
  • Search queries and their results
  • Which scripture passages you open, mark, or annotate
  • Speaker and sermon metadata you enter or that is derived on your device
  • Your settings, including language, appearance, reminder, and voice preferences

Recording, transcription, summarisation, indexing, search, and scripture analysis are all performed on your device. Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Read-aloud uses a neural speech model that runs on your device.

iCloud and CloudKit synchronisation are disabled in the App. Your library is not backed up to any server we control. It may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud device backup if you have device backup enabled. Such backups are controlled by Apple and are governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Because we hold no server-side copy of your library, deleting the App removes its locally stored library data from the device. We cannot restore that library for you. Section 9 explains how to export it first.

4. When the App uses the network, and what is sent

4.1 Downloading the on-device speech model

The first time the App prepares read-aloud, it downloads a neural text-to-speech model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which hosts the model files.

This is an ordinary file download. The request carries standard network information such as your IP address and connection metadata, which is handled by Hugging Face under its own privacy policy.

No sermon, transcript, note, or scripture content is sent to Hugging Face as part of this model download.

Once the model is installed, read-aloud runs locally on the device.

4.2 Apple speech assets

Apple's speech recognition and system voices may download language or speech assets through iOS. These requests are handled by the operating system and are governed by Apple's privacy practices.

4.3 Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we do not receive your full payment card details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify and manage subscription entitlements.

RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous application user identifier together with transaction and entitlement information associated with the App Store purchase, such as which product was purchased, whether the entitlement is active, and relevant subscription status information.

We do not operate an Afterlife account system, and we do not intentionally attach your name or e-mail address to this RevenueCat identifier.

This processing is used to provide paid features and support subscription entitlement and purchase restoration.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy .

4.4 Usage statistics and crash reports

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the App is used and to diagnose defects.

What may be collected:

  • Which screens are opened and the order in which they are used
  • Which features are used — for example, that a recording was started, read-aloud was used, a search was run, or the subscription screen was opened or dismissed
  • The outcome of operations — for example, whether a purchase succeeded, processing completed, or the speech model installed
  • Coarse magnitudes expressed as ranges rather than exact values. For example, a recording may be reported as falling within a duration range rather than reporting its precise duration.
  • App or installation characteristics such as subscription entitlement status, selected read-aloud engine, appearance setting, and library-size range
  • Technical information Firebase may collect, such as an app instance identifier, vendor identifier (IDFV) where applicable, device model, operating system version, App version, language, and approximate country or region information
  • Crash and diagnostic information such as stack traces, device or application state, and internal error codes

What our custom Analytics events do not include:

Our custom Analytics event parameters are designed to use fixed developer-defined values rather than user-entered free text. We do not intentionally send the following content through our custom Analytics events:

  • Sermon audio or transcript content
  • Sermon titles, speaker names, or pastor names
  • Your notes, highlights, or annotations
  • Your search query text
  • The specific book, chapter, or verse of scripture you are reading
  • Names of files you import
  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number, or contacts
  • Your precise location

What we do not do:

  • We do not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for advertising or tracking.
  • The App does not use an advertising SDK.
  • Google Signals, advertising personalisation, advertising storage, and advertising conversion measurement are disabled in our Analytics configuration.
  • We do not assign a custom Analytics user ID tied to an Afterlife account because the App does not operate an account system.
  • We do not use this data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Your control. Usage statistics can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics.

Turning this setting off stops future Firebase Analytics collection by the App on that device. Previously collected Analytics data remains subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion controls.

Crash reporting remains enabled so that application defects can be diagnosed. Crash reports may include technical information about the application and device state at the time of a failure.

Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Firebase privacy information is available at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Because the App has no account system and we hold no server-side copy of your sermon library, many controls relating to your library are available directly in the App:

  • Access and portability. Settings › Privacy › Export allows you to create an export of your library that you can save or share.
  • Erasure. Settings › Privacy › Delete all data permanently removes the App's locally stored sermon library and associated local data from the device.
  • Objection to usage statistics. Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics stops future Firebase Analytics collection by the App on that device.

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

Residents of California and certain other US states may also have rights regarding personal information under applicable state privacy laws.

We do not sell personal information or use personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To make a privacy request that cannot be completed directly in the App, contact supportafterlifeapp@gmail.com .

Because technical Analytics identifiers are not associated with an Afterlife account or identity that we maintain, we may not always be able to identify a specific Analytics record as belonging to a particular individual.

11. Security

Your locally stored content is protected by iOS application sandboxing and by the security mechanisms provided by your device and operating system.

Network communications used by the App and its service providers use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections where supported by the relevant services.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your sermon library, which limits the amount of user-created sermon content available through our own systems.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
